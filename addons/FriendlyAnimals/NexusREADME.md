[font=Verdana][size=5][color=#1e84cc][b]What it names[/b][/color][/size][/font]
[list]
[*][font=Verdana][b]Brahmin[/b] [color=#a0a0a0]- pack, water, malnourished and Fiend brahmin, and calves[/color][/font]
[*][font=Verdana][b]Bighorners[/b] [color=#a0a0a0]- bulls and calves, and the mountain bighorners of Honest Hearts. They graze, and only fight when you get close[/color][/font]
[*][font=Verdana][b]Guard dogs[/b] [color=#a0a0a0]- Legion, NCR, Brotherhood, raider, Fiend and Talon Company dogs, ranch dogs, and prospectors' and scavengers' dogs[/color][/font]
[*][font=Verdana][b]Games[/b] [color=#a0a0a0]- New Vegas, its DLCs and the Fallout 3 side of TTW[/color][/font]
[*][font=Verdana][b]Name lists[/b] [color=#a0a0a0]- 45 brahmin, 33 bighorner and 50 dog names. None of them is a name the games already give a character[/color][/font]
[/list]
[font=Verdana][b]Not named:[/b] wild animals that hunt you (Vicious Dog, Wild Dog, Coyote), and animals that already have their own name (Rex, Dogmeat, Betsy the Brahmin).[/font]
