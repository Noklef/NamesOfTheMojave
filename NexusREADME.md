[center][color=#1e84cc][font=Verdana][size=6][b]Names of the Mojave[/b][/size][/font][/color][/center]
[font=Verdana][b][i]Names of the Mojave[/i][/b] is an ESP-less mod that gives the generic NPCs of the wasteland their own names.[/font]
[font=Verdana][i]Powder Ganger[/i] becomes [i]Henry Glover[/i], [i]Recruit Legionary[/i] becomes [i]Cassius[/i], and so on.[/font]

[font=Verdana]Unique NPCs are never touched. [i]Easy Pete[/i] stays [i]Easy Pete[/i] (unless you want him to).[/font]

[center][color=#1e84cc][font=Verdana][size=5][b]Features[/b][/size][/font][/color][/center]
[list]
[*][font=Verdana][b][color=#1e84cc]ESP-less[/color][/b]: no extra plugin in your load order[/font][/*]
[*][font=Verdana][b][color=#1e84cc]A name for every generic NPC[/color][/b], even when 50 of them share one record. Gender-aware, with unisex names, and no two NPCs nearby share a name[/font][/*]
[*][font=Verdana][b][color=#1e84cc]Consistent[/color][/b]: the same name for the whole playthrough, across saves and reloads. A new game gives new names[/font][/*]
[*][font=Verdana][b][color=#1e84cc]Dialogue too[/color][/b]: the dialogue window shows the new name[/font][/*]
[*][font=Verdana][i][b][color=#1e84cc]TTW Compatible[/color][/b][/i]: New Vegas, its DLCs, and the Fallout 3 side of TTW. On plain New Vegas the Fallout 3 content is skipped[/font][/*]
[*][font=Verdana][b][color=#1e84cc]287 roles, 8 name pools, nearly 1,500 names[/color][/b]: Wasteland, Legion (Latin), Ghoul, Tribal, Chinese, Super mutant, Swampfolk and Kids[/font][/*]
[*][font=Verdana][b][color=#1e84cc]Configurable[/color][/b] through [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/newvegas/mods/93642]MCM Extender[/url] (optional) or the INI:[/font]
[list]
[*][font=Verdana][b]Enabled[/b]: off gives named NPCs their original names back[/font][/*]
[*][font=Verdana][b]Show role after name[/b]: [i]Henry Glover (Powder Ganger)[/i][/font][/*]
[*][font=Verdana][b]Use titles[/b]: [i]Ranger Barker[/i] (on) or [i]Erik Barker[/i] (off)[/font][/*]
[*][font=Verdana][b]Scan interval[/b]: how often to look for new NPCs (default 10 seconds)[/font][/*]
[*][font=Verdana][b]Name seed[/b] (MCM only): change it to give every NPC a new name[/font][/*]
[/list]
[/*]
[*][font=Verdana][b][color=#1e84cc]Add-on support[/color][/b]: other mods' NPCs can be named with drop-in text files (see Add-ons)[/font][/*]
[*][font=Verdana][b][color=#1e84cc]Save friendly[/color][/b]: only one number (the playthrough seed) goes in your save, so uninstalling is clean[/font][/*]
[/list]

[center][color=#1e84cc][font=Verdana][size=5][b]Compatibility Notes[/b][/size][/font][/color][/center]
[font=Verdana]This is a very save-friendly mod, and can 100% be installed mid-game. The only thing that is saved to a save file is the random seed used to generate the names (literally a single variable). In regards to other things though:[/font]
[list]
[*][font=Verdana][b]Mods that rename NPCs[/b]: Should be fine. An NPC whose name already differs from its record is left alone.[/font][/*]
[*][font=Verdana][b]Mods that add NPCs[/b]: not named unless there's an add-on for them (or they reuse a vanilla NPC record).[/font][/*]
[*][font=Verdana][b]Translated games[/b]: rules match the in-game name, so a translated game needs a rules file in its own language ([i]translation add-ons welcome[/i]).[/font][/*]
[*][font=Verdana][b]Changing name lists mid-playthrough[/b]: This will cause a re-roll of the names that use those lists.[/font][/*]
[*][font=Verdana][b]Ghouls and children in mixed groups[/b] (e.g. ghoul Wastelanders, child Refugees) get wasteland names: the pool follows the role, not the race.[/font][/*]
[/list]
[font=Verdana]If something looks off, check [i][b]NamesOfTheMojave.log[/b][/i] (in the game folder, or [i]overwrite/Root/[/i] with MO2 Root Builder). It lists the load summary, rules that add-ons replaced or removed, bad rule lines, and every NPC that was named.[/font]
